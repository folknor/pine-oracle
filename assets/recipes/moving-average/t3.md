# T3 Moving Average

Tillson's T3 is a smooth, low-lag average built from six chained EMAs combined
with a volume-factor weighting. The volume factor (0 to 1) trades smoothness for
responsiveness. There is no `ta.t3()` builtin.

## Recipe

```pine
//@version=6
indicator("T3 Moving Average", "T3", overlay = true)

length  = input.int(8, "Length", minval = 1)
vfactor = input.float(0.7, "Volume Factor", minval = 0, maxval = 1)
src     = input.source(close, "Source")

t3(source, len, v) =>
    e1 = ta.ema(source, len)
    e2 = ta.ema(e1, len)
    e3 = ta.ema(e2, len)
    e4 = ta.ema(e3, len)
    e5 = ta.ema(e4, len)
    e6 = ta.ema(e5, len)
    c1 = -v * v * v
    c2 = 3 * v * v + 3 * v * v * v
    c3 = -6 * v * v - 3 * v - 3 * v * v * v
    c4 = 1 + 3 * v + v * v * v + 3 * v * v
    c1 * e6 + c2 * e5 + c3 * e4 + c4 * e3

plot(t3(src, length, vfactor), "T3", color.orange, 2)
```

## How to read it

The T3 is smoother than an EMA yet turns faster, which makes it a good trend
ribbon or signal line. A lower volume factor smooths more (more lag); a higher
one tracks price more aggressively. Read it by slope and by price crossing it.
